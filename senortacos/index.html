Our Story
Born in Mexico.
Raised in Lafayette.

Señor Tacos is a family-owned restaurant where the owner, his wife, and their family work side-by-side as cooks, servers, and cashiers. Born and raised in Mexico, they've called Lafayette home since 2001.

Every recipe is authentic — passed down through generations. Every salsa made fresh each morning. Every customer treated like family.
